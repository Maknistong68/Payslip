1

Download the Excel template

Fill the Settings sheet once (company name, CR number, pay period…) and one row per employee in the Payslips sheet. Keep the header row as it is.

Tips for filling the template
  • Amounts must be numbers. Empty cell = 0.
  • Extra pay items: add a column named Earning: Site Allowance. Extra deductions: Deduction: Housing Loan. Any name works.
  • Net salary is calculated for you (earnings − deductions). The optional Net Salary column is only cross-checked.
  • Delete the optional columns you do not need. Required: Employee ID, Employee Name, Basic Salary.
  • Use English / Latin letters. Arabic text is not supported by the PDF fonts in this version.
  • Next month: change the Pay Period and the amounts, upload again. Logo and design are remembered on this computer.
